Our Legacy

A Tradition of Excellence

O P Jindal School, established in 1996 under the aegis of the Jindal Education Trust, has grown into one of the most respected CBSE-affiliated institutions in Haryana.

Named after the visionary industrialist and philanthropist Shri Om Prakash Jindal, the school embodies his belief that quality education is the foundation of national progress. Our sprawling campus in Kharkhoda features modern laboratories, expansive sports grounds, smart classrooms, and nurturing environments designed to unlock the full potential of every child.

Our Mission

To provide world-class education that empowers students to become ethical leaders and innovative thinkers in a rapidly changing world.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence nurturing global citizens grounded in Indian values, integrity, and the spirit of inquiry.

Core Values

Integrity, Excellence, Compassion, Innovation, and Respect — the pillars that guide every decision and interaction.
